Language Reports:
The language reports are due as indicated in the calendar below (labelled LgRep). You must submit one language report from Area 1, one from Area 2, and one from Area 3. You do not have to submit them in this order, though. For example, your first language report could be from Area 2, your second language report could be from Area 3, then your last report would be from Area 1. Here is a sample of a language report.
  1. Area 1 - Indo-European (other than English)
  2. Area 2 - East or Southeast Asian (other than Korean, Japanese, or Mandarin)
  3. Area 3 - Any language family other than the ones above
