This workshop will cover grammatical aspects on a variety of minority languages spoken in Asia including Drenjongke (Sino-Tibetan), Kiribati (Austronesian), and Tamang (Sino-Tibetan). There will also be two short elicitation sessions at noon on Drenjongke and Kiribati available to students who wish to practice their elicitation skills. The sessions will focus on the elicitation of syntactic and semantic phenomena. The elicitation sessions are by registration only. Please email Michael Barrie at mikebarrie@sogang.ac.kr.
