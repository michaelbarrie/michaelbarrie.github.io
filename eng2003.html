Phonology (flapping): Listen to this video and to this video. In the first video a child star is about to sing a song suggested by a boy in the audience. In the second video, the child star is now older and is hallucinating she is a young child singing the same song. The line is, "I've written a letter to daddy." Try to listen for differences in flapping between the three speakers saying this line:

Boy: [aɪ̯v ɹɪʔn̩ ə lεɾɹ̩ ɾə dæɾi]

Girl: [aɪ̯v ɹɪʔn̩ ɐ lεɾɹ̩ tʰu dædi]

Woman: [aɪ̯v ɹɪʔn̩ ɐ lεtɹ̩ tʰu dædi]

Phonology (Korean rules): Listen to this video and try to figure out what rules of Korean phonology the people in the video are talking about? Try to write the rules.
  • at 2:24 - obstruents become nasals before another nasal
  • at 5:18 - obstruent nasalization again; variation in lateral nasalization (/l/ → [n] / [+nas] __ (quite variable). Some speakers pronounce 종로 as 종노 (Standard Korean), some do not. Note there is a special, highly lexicalized rule if /l/ follows /n/.
  • at 9:02 - Consonant cluster simplification
