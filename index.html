Michael Barrie



I am a professor in the English Department at Sogang University. I am interested in syntactic theory, the syntax-semantics interface, and the syntax-prosody interface. I have worked mostly on Iroquoian and on Algonquian, but have also worked in English, Cantonese, Korean, Tamang, Tagalog, Mongolian, and Romance.
